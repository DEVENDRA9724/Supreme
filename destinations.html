Canada Immigration & Study

Express Entry • Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP) • SDS Student Visa

Canada remains the premier destination for skilled immigrants, professionals, and students from Gujarat. With a target of welcoming over 500,000 permanent residents annually, Canada offers clear pathways from education to post-graduation work permits (PGWP) and permanent residency.

Express Entry Streams

  • • Federal Skilled Worker (FSW): Minimum 67/100 points threshold.
  • • Canadian Experience Class (CEC): For candidates with 1 year Canadian work.
  • • Category Draws: Tech, Healthcare, Trades, and French speakers.

Provincial Nominee Programs

  • • Ontario (OINP): Human Capital & Tech Streams (600 Bonus Points).
  • • Alberta (AAIP): Accelerated Tech Pathway & Rural Renewal.
  • • British Columbia (BC PNP): Tech Stream & International Post-Graduate.
Study in Canada (SDS): Fast-track processing with PAL (Provincial Attestation Letter), GIC verification, and up to 3 years Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP).

Canada Quick Facts

Average Processing: 6 - 8 Months
Min. Language Requirement: CLB 7 (IELTS 6 each)
Post-Study Work Permit: Up to 3 Years
Spouse Open Work Permit: Eligible (Masters/PhD)

Australia Skilled Migration & Study

General Skilled Migration (GSM 189/190/491) • Subclass 500 Student Visa

Australia offers world-class living standards, high wages, and transparent points-based permanent residency pathways. With Supreme Immigration's MARA-aligned advisory, your skills assessment and visa lodgement are conducted with strict legal adherence.

Visa Subclasses

  • • Subclass 189: Independent Skilled PR (Direct federal grant).
  • • Subclass 190: State Nominated (+5 bonus points).
  • • Subclass 491: Regional Sponsored (+15 bonus points, 5-yr PR pathway).

Study in Australia

  • • Group of Eight (Go8) & Top Tech Unis: Melbourne, Sydney, UNSW, Monash.
  • • Subclass 485 (PSW): Up to 2 - 4 years post-study work authorization.
  • • Genuine Student (GS) Criterion: Expert drafting of financial & career proof.

Australia Quick Facts

Points Pass Mark: Minimum 65 Points
Skill Authorities: ACS, EA, VETASSESS
English Standard: IELTS 7 / PTE 65+
Compliance: MARA Code of Conduct

United Kingdom (UK) Opportunities

Tier 4 Student Visa • Graduate Route (PSW) • Skilled Worker

The UK remains one of the world's most prestigious academic destinations, offering 1-year Master's programs that save both time and living expenses. Following graduation, candidates receive 2 years on the Graduate Route (PSW) to secure employer sponsorship under the Skilled Worker Visa.

Top UK Institutions

  • • Russell Group Universities admissions.
  • • Generous scholarships up to £5,000 - £10,000.
  • • Fast CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) turnaround.

Post-Study Career Route

  • • 2 Years Graduate Route work authorization.
  • • Transition to Skilled Worker Visa sponsorship.
  • • Direct 5-year Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) path.

UK Quick Facts

Degree Duration: 1 Year Masters
IELTS Requirement: 6.0 - 6.5 (Waivers Avail)
Intakes: Sept / Jan / May

Germany & Schengen Europe

Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card) • Zero Tuition Universities • EU Blue Card

Germany offers exceptional engineering, IT, and business careers with virtually ZERO tuition fees at public universities and the innovative points-based Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) for job seekers.

Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)

  • • Minimum 6 points requirement based on qualification, language, age.
  • • Live and work up to 1-2 years in Germany while searching for full-time job.
  • • Conversion into EU Blue Card upon employment contract.

Public University Admissions

  • • 100% Free Tuition degrees taught in English.
  • • Blocked account (Sperrkonto) setup assistance.
  • • APS Certificate document validation support.

Germany Highlights

Tuition Fees: €0 at Public Unis
Chancenkarte Points: Min 6 Points
